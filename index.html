Questions, answered.

What types of websites do you create?

We create business websites, personalized greeting websites for birthdays and anniversaries, and digital invitations for weddings and other events.

How do I start a project?

Fill in the enquiry form below. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send, and we will reply to discuss your idea.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the pages, features and design you need. We agree on the price with you before work begins.

Are domain and hosting charges included?

Not automatically. A domain name and hosting are separate services. We can explain the options and what each would cost, and include setup in the agreed scope if you wish.

Can I request changes to the design?

Yes. We share progress for feedback, and the number of revision rounds is agreed in the project scope.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

How long does a website take to complete?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly feedback arrives. We agree a timeline with you during planning.

Can you create personalized greeting websites?

Yes. Share your message, photos and the occasion, and we will design a greeting website around them.

Can I request updates after launch?

Yes. Updates and maintenance are available, and their terms and cost are agreed with you for each project.
